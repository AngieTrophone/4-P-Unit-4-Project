My Unofficial Local Guide to Coney Island, Brooklyn

Pizza, Ice Cream, and other food areas

Food is a very important thing in Coney Island. The best one's I've been to when getting different kinds of meals and desserts come from

    Luigi's Pizzaria
    Maestro Breakfast cafe
    Masuta Sushi restraunt
    and Haggen Daaz Ice Cream


The attractions

A big part of Coney Island is the iconic area of rides known by all as Luna Park, home to the Cyclone. This park holds rides for all ages! Ranging from fast rides like the Thunderbolt and Slingshot, all the way to the rides such as the teacup and the carousel.
